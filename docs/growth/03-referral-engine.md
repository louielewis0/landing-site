# Referral Engine — Real Estate Market Center

**Why:** Referrals convert at 15–25% vs. 0.4–1.2% for cold online leads — the best conversion of any
source. You already have the raw material: happy clients and a 5.0 rating across 70+ Google reviews.
You just need the system to ask, consistently.

> ⚠️ **Compliance note (have your attorney confirm):** In Michigan you generally **cannot pay a cash
> referral fee to an unlicensed person** (only to licensed agents/brokers). So reward client referrals
> with **thoughtful thank-you gifts and genuine appreciation**, not cash. Any paid co-marketing with a
> lender/title company must comply with **RESPA** (fair market value, no pay-for-referrals). Keep it clean.

---

## Part A — the post-closing sequence (review + referral)

The best time to ask is right after a great experience. Run this on every closing.

### 1. Review ask — 24–72 hours after closing (text)
> Hi [First name] — congratulations again on your [new home / sale]! It was a genuine pleasure
> working with you. If you have 60 seconds, would you mind leaving a quick Google review? It helps
> other families find us. Here's the direct link: [your Google review link]. Thank you! — Sundus

*(This feeds your review engine — velocity of new reviews matters most for ranking.)*

### 2. Referral ask — ~2 weeks later, after they've settled (text or call)
> Hi [First name], hope you're getting settled in! One quick thing — my business runs almost entirely
> on referrals from happy clients like you, not on advertising. If a friend, family member, or
> coworker ever mentions buying or selling, I'd be grateful if you'd pass along my name. I'll take
> great care of them, same as I did for you. Thank you! — Sundus (248) 568-6081

### 3. Stay top-of-mind (ongoing)
- Add them to the monthly newsletter (02-database-newsletter.md).
- **Home-anniversary** text each year: "Happy 1 year in your [area] home, [name]! Hope it still feels
  like home. If you ever need anything — or know someone who does — I'm here."
- A small **closing gift** and an occasional personal pop-by/note keep you referable.

## Part B — the referral program (one-pager)

Make it easy and memorable for clients to refer you. Simple framing:

> **The Real Estate Market Center Referral Promise**
> We grow by referral, not advertising — which means we have to earn it every time.
> - Refer a friend or family member who's buying or selling, and we'll take care of them personally,
>   with the same straight advice and service you got.
> - As a thank-you for every referral that closes with us, you'll get [a thoughtful gift —
>   e.g., a nice dinner/gift card / a local experience]. *(Gifts, not cash — keeps it compliant.)*
> - It's the best compliment you can give us, and we never forget it.
>
> Just send them our way: Sundus Lewis, (248) 568-6081 · marketcenterrealty.com

Put this on a card in your closing gift, in your email signature, and mention it at every closing.

## Part C — lender / title / vendor partnerships (the highest-converting buyer source)

Agent-referred mortgage leads fund at ~15–25% vs 2–4% for paid leads. Build **3–5 trusted partners**
and send value first. (Again: no pay-for-referrals — RESPA. Co-marketing must be fair-value.)

**Who to build relationships with:** 2–3 local lenders (one great on first-time-buyer / MSHDA
programs), 1 title/settlement company, plus handy vendors (inspector, stager, contractor, insurance)
you can confidently refer clients to.

### Lender partner outreach (email or DM)
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm Sundus Lewis, broker/owner of Real Estate Market Center in Troy. I'm looking to build a short
> list of lenders I can confidently refer my buyers to — people who respond fast, close on time, and
> treat clients the way I do. I send a lot of first-time buyers and could use a strong partner on
> MSHDA / down-payment-assistance deals especially.
>
> Would you be open to a quick call to see if we're a fit? I value responsiveness and honesty over
> everything.
>
> Sundus Lewis · (248) 568-6081 · marketcenterrealty.com

### What a good partnership looks like
- **You send them** pre-approval-ready buyers; **they send you** buyers who got pre-approved but have
  no agent (hugely valuable — these convert at 15–25%).
- Co-host a **first-time-buyer workshop** (pairs perfectly with your /michigan-down-payment-assistance
  and /first-time-home-buyer-programs-michigan pages).
- Cross-refer only people you'd stake your name on — one bad referral costs the relationship.

## The through-line
Reviews → referrals → database → more referrals. Every happy closing should produce (1) a review,
(2) a referral ask, and (3) a permanent spot in your newsletter. That loop, run consistently, becomes
your primary pipeline — cheaper and higher-converting than any paid lead.
