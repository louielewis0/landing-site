**Where values stand (year-over-year, Zillow home-value index)**
Every suburb we track is **up** from a year ago — none is declining:

- **Birmingham** — typical home ~$749K, **+6.5%** (the hottest market right now; Redfin sale prices +13.6%)

- **Rochester Hills** — ~$470K, **+3.5%** — and the **fastest-selling** (median 14 days)
- **Troy** — ~$459K, **+2.2%** (median 19 days)
- **West Bloomfield** — ~$457K, **+2.8%**
- **Sterling Heights** — ~$309K, **+1.4%** (15 days)
- **Warren** — ~$201K, **+0.4%** (21 days)

**The one-line takeaway:** well-priced homes are still selling in **2–4 weeks** across the board, and the luxury end (Birmingham/Bloomfield) is running hotter than the affordable end this cycle.
