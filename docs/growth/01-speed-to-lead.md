Responding in 5 min = ~21x more likely to qualify the lead. Most agents take 15 hours and never
follow up past the first try — this is your cheapest edge.

and an email. Text first (85% open in minutes); email as backup with a real next step.

## Step 2 — the 12-touch follow-up cadence

80% of deals close between the 5th and 12th touch. 65% of leads need 6+ months of nurture. Don't
rely on memory — put this in the CRM as an automated sequence and personalize the first and last few.

| # | When | Channel | Message |
|---|------|---------|---------|
| 1 | 0–5 min | Text | Instant text (above) |
| 2 | 0–5 min | Email | Instant email (above) |
| 3 | Same day | Call + VM | Voicemail (above) |
| 4 | Day 2 | Text | "Hi [name] — still happy to help with [area]. Want me to send a few homes in your range / a free valuation? Just say the word." |
| 5 | Day 4 | Email | Send value: 2–3 matching listings (buyer) OR the net-proceeds calculator (seller): marketcenterrealty.com/home-sale-proceeds-calculator |
| 6 | Day 7 | Call | Quick check-in; leave VM if missed |
| 7 | Day 11 | Text | "No pressure [name] — when you're ready to look, I'm here. Anything I can answer in the meantime?" |
| 8 | Day 18 | Email | Local market update / relevant guide (e.g., school-district or down-payment-assistance page) |
| 9 | Day 30 | Call | Monthly touch |
| 10 | Day 45 | Text | Personal, light: "Thinking of you [name] — any change in your timeline?" |
| 11 | Day 60 | Email | Add to monthly newsletter (see 02-database-newsletter.md) and note it |
| 12 | Day 75+ | Call/Text | "Should I keep you on my monthly Metro Detroit market update, or circle back in a few months?" |

After touch 12, move them to the **monthly newsletter** (long-term nurture) — never delete a lead.

### Buyer vs. seller value to send
- **Buyer:** 2–3 matching listings, the affordability calculator (/home-affordability-calculator),
  down-payment-assistance checker (/michigan-down-payment-assistance), the relevant city/school guide.
- **Seller:** the free valuation (/home-value), net-proceeds calculator
  (/home-sale-proceeds-calculator), a "just sold near you" note, the market-update page.
