# Database / SOI Engine + Monthly Newsletter — Real Estate Market Center

**Why this is the #1 ROI move:** ~66% of sellers and ~40% of buyers come via referral or a past
relationship — but ~93% of past clients use a *different* agent next time, almost always because the
agent went quiet. Staying in front of your database is the highest-return, lowest-cost thing you can do.

**The cadence:** ~33–36 touches/year (about 3/month), mixed channels. The monthly newsletter is one of
those touches — the rest are personal (text, call, note, pop-by on birthdays/home-anniversaries).

---

## Step 1 — build the database

Load EVERY contact into one place (your CRM, or a clean spreadsheet to start):
- Past clients (every closing — these are gold)
- Current + past leads
- Sphere: friends, family, neighbors, vendors, anyone who knows you

**Tag each by:** relationship (past client / sphere / lead), type (buyer / seller / investor),
area, price band, and home-anniversary date (for past clients).

---

## Step 2 — the monthly market-update newsletter

One send a month, minimum. Use a simple tool (Mailchimp, Beehiiv, or your CRM's email). Pull the
numbers from marketcenterrealty.com/metro-detroit-housing-market-update.

**Subject line options (rotate):**
- "Metro Detroit market update — [Month]: what's actually happening"
- "[Month] in Oakland & Macomb: prices, days on market, and 1 thing buyers are missing"
- "Is it a buyer's or seller's market right now? ([Month] numbers)"

## Step 3 — segment the sends

Don't blast everyone the same thing. Three simple lists:

- **Sellers / homeowners:** lead with value (what their home is worth), the net-proceeds calculator,
  "just sold near you" proof, the sell-vs-rent tool.
- **Buyers:** new/matching listings, affordability + down-payment tools, school-district and
  neighborhood guides.
- **Past clients:** mostly relationship — home-anniversary note, "happy to be your resource,"
  referral-friendly value (see 03-referral-engine.md). They're your referral machine, not a sales target.

## Step 4 — the 12-month value-touch calendar (beyond the newsletter)

Mix in personal, non-salesy touches so you hit ~3/month total:

| Month | Extra touch idea |
|-------|------------------|
| Jan | "New year, new market" outlook + personal hello |
| Feb | Spring-market prep tips (sellers) |
| Mar | Property-tax / assessment reminder (Michigan) |
| Apr | Spring market in full swing — just-listed roundup |
| May | Home-maintenance checklist |
| Jun | Mid-year market check-in |
| Jul | Lake-living / summer lifestyle content (your lakefront guides!) |
| Aug | Back-to-school → school-district guides |
| Sep | Fall market + "is now a good time?" |
| Oct | Home-anniversary notes to past clients |
| Nov | Gratitude note (Thanksgiving) — pure relationship, no ask |
| Dec | Year-in-review + "here's what [area] did in 2026" |

Plus year-round: **birthday + home-anniversary texts** to past clients (highest-value personal touch).
