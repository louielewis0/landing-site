### Self-serve today — do these first

**1. Yelp** — free, ~24h approval. Go to https://biz.yelp.com/claim → search
"Real Estate Market Center" (agent-level pages exist; if no brokerage page,
"Add to Yelp for free") → verify by email or text/call to the listed phone.
Yelp states claiming is free and most pages are listed within 24 hours.

**2. ThreeBestRated** — free self-submission, reviewed within 7 business days.
https://threebestrated.com/submit-business?reason=new — 4-step form (contact,
preferences, achievements, business info). Have ready: hours, services,
descriptions (use this kit), images, and **licensing docs** (they ask). Their

business owners to submit their businesses." Note: Troy has no real-estate
category page yet — the submit form uses a city dropdown, so whether an
unlisted city works is unconfirmed; submit and see.

a REALTOR-association page — secondhand; the portal itself is login-walled.)
