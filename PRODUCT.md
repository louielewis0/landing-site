# PRODUCT.md — Market Center Realty

## What this is
Two surfaces in one Next.js app:
1. **marketcenterrealty.com** — public brokerage site for a family-run Metro Detroit real estate brokerage ("Real Estate Market Center", Troy MI, 20 years in business). Lead capture (hero form, home-value tool, contact) feeds the CRM.
2. **/crm** — the brokerage's own passcode-gated CRM: lead pipeline, expired-listing prospecting machine (Matrix MLS import, skip trace via Enformion, DNC scrubber, lead scoring), activity logging, playbook. This is proprietary internal software, being built toward the thesis "the brokerage IS the AI/software layer its agents can't leave."

## Register
`product` for /crm (design serves daily work); `brand` for the public site.

## Users
- **Louie** — founder-operator, works expired-listing prospecting daily (call blocks, follow-ups, showings). Power user, impatient, wants zero friction and obvious next actions.
- **Family/agents** (near future) — licensed agents of the brokerage; less technical, will judge the brokerage partly by how premium its internal tools feel.
- Sessions: desk (large screen) for morning import/triage; phone in the field between knocks and showings.

## Tone / brand
Dark luxury, old-money restraint: near-black warm inks, bone (warm off-white) text, muted gold accents used sparingly. Serif display (Cormorant Garamond) for headlines, sans (Geist) for UI. Feels like a private club's ledger, not a SaaS tool. Confidence through restraint; data-first, no hype (owner explicitly dislikes salesy/influencer aesthetics).

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards (blue/white, identical stat cards, gradient text).
- Fitness-influencer / crypto-bro neon hype.
- Cluttered legacy real-estate tools (Top Producer-era density, beige Windows-form energy).

## Strategic principles
- The CRM's daily screen is a prioritized work queue, not a stats page.
- Every surface answers "what's next and when" (next-activity discipline).
- Compliance is wired in (DNC/TCPA reminders live in the UI, not a manual).
- Premium feel is a recruiting weapon: agents should *want* to show this to peers.
